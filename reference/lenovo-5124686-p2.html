Re:Yoga X1 OLED Gen 2 OLED screen failure support request

I can confirm the winter theory.

I have qty 3 "X1 yoga gen2 oled" laptops.

Because I have never seen a better laptop ever (but there are starting to be good alternatives).

one can stay flicker / non-corrupted for a few weeks.

The other two only last a few days.

Once the screen becomes corrupt l drain the battery and leave it on the shelf for a few weeks.

After a few weeks the screen becomes good again.

The humidity does seem to affect the screen, I had always thought it was a corrupt driver IC, that cannot be reset through software, but the AFC tape theory seems plausible also.
